Energy 101

Megawatts and megawatt-hours

A megawatt (MW) measures power at a point in time. A megawatt-hour (MWh) measures energy. A 100 MW facility running at full output for ten hours produces 1,000 MWh.

Nuclear fuel and generation

Uranium is mined and processed into fuel before it reaches a reactor. Contracting for fuel, operating a reactor and building a new reactor are different businesses with different risks.

Natural gas and power

Producers extract gas, pipelines move it and power plants burn it to generate electricity. Gas plants can adjust output as demand changes, subject to fuel availability and operating constraints.

Renewables and storage

Wind and solar output varies with weather and daylight. Batteries can shift energy from one hour to another. Their power and storage duration determine how large a gap they can cover.

The electrical grid

Generation feeds high-voltage transmission lines. Substations and transformers lower the voltage for distribution to customers. A utility can have access to generation and still face a local grid bottleneck.

Power purchase agreements

A PPA sets terms for buying electricity. Pay attention to when deliveries begin, how prices are set and whether the contract depends on permits, construction or interconnection.
